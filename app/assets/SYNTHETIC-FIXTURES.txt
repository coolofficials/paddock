Schedule fixtures in this directory are hand-authored parser and layout test data.
They are not an official iRacing schedule and must not be used to plan races.
The first eight series identifiers and one circuit name exercise legacy compatibility.
All weekly arrangements, remaining series, cars, and circuit layouts are invented.
The fixture year remains 2026 solely for deterministic date-boundary regression tests.
Normal public startup does not load these fixtures. Refresh downloads the official
schedule into the user's private application data directory.
